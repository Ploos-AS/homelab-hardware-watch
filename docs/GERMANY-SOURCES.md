# Germany sources

## ServerShop24

Public catalogue with explicit stock counts, VAT-inclusive EUR prices and refurbished server/workstation/storage inventory.

Important: displayed prices include German VAT and exclude shipping. Delivery-time statements shown on the site apply to Germany; Norway shipping must remain unknown until explicitly resolved.

Interesting classes include Dell/HPE/Fujitsu/Lenovo servers, ZFS-ready systems, storage arrays and workstations.

## Serverando

Public catalogue with explicit stock, VAT-inclusive EUR prices and shipping excluded. Inventory includes complete servers, configurable systems, components, storage and networking.

Because Serverando mixes complete devices and configurable products, M2 initially marks this source configurable. A later product-detail pass can distinguish fixed configurations from configurator bases.

## Policy

German local-VAT prices are observations, not Norwegian delivered prices. Do not compare them directly with the N150 NOK baseline until M3 import/tax/currency handling exists.
