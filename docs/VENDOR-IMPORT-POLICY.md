# Vendor import policy

Delivered-cost comparison must distinguish verified facts from missing purchase terms.

Policy states are intentionally conservative:

- `confirmed`: explicitly verified for Norway.
- `quote`: value must come from an explicit vendor quote.
- `checkout`: value must be captured from a Norway checkout.
- `unknown`: no reliable evidence has been recorded.

A catalogue price is never enough by itself to calculate a delivered Norway price.

For configurable systems, the final configured price must also be known before economic ranking.

The initial policies deliberately leave normal EU storefronts as `unknown` until their Norway purchase flow is verified. SecondHandServer is treated as quote-driven and requires explicit quote values.

Future observations should record evidence date and source so a vendor policy can change without rewriting historical candidate data.
