# GEKKO Computer

GEKKO exposes a large public refurbished-server catalogue with explicit model/configuration titles, stock counts and EUR prices.

## M2 treatment

- automatic catalogue source
- country: DE
- preserve displayed EUR price
- stock is useful for opportunity freshness
- product titles often contain CPU count/model, RAM, controller and bay layout
- do not infer Norwegian delivered price

The catalogue is especially useful for configured Dell PowerEdge, HPE ProLiant, Fujitsu Primergy and Lenovo ThinkSystem systems.

GEKKO also states that systems can be configured to customer requirements. M2 treats fully specified catalogue rows as concrete observations; custom-request pricing remains outside automatic comparison.
