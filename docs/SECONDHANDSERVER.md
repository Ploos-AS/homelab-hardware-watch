# SecondHandServer.eu

SecondHandServer.eu is treated as an RFQ/manual sourcing source rather than a public-price collector.

## Verified source characteristics

- buys and sells servers across Europe
- explicitly lists Norway among served markets
- rack/tower/blade servers plus routers, switches and accessories
- Dell R540/R640/R740/R840/R940 and HP ProLiant families are among listed stock families
- Cisco switch families are also listed
- warranty and tested used hardware are core parts of the offer
- shipping/logistics references include major parcel carriers

## Watch policy

The site exposes product families and stock-oriented sourcing information, but not a dependable structured public price feed suitable for automatic price comparison.

Therefore:

- never invent an item price
- represent the source as an RFQ/manual lead
- capture an actual quote later as a dated marketplace observation
- preserve exact configuration, warranty, shipping and tax terms from the quote
- a quote can enter opportunity scoring only after those fields are known

This source is especially relevant for Proxmox/server, storage and managed-networking RFQs.
